About BookMyTrack

Travel Made Inclusive

BookMyTrack is a modern railway reservation platform designed to make train travel in India more inclusive, comfortable, and user-friendly. Our platform enhances the traditional IRCTC system by addressing long-standing challenges such as gender inclusivity, seating issues, and accessibility for elderly passengers.

Our Purpose

The purpose of BookMyTrack is to provide a railway booking experience that respects and accommodates passengers of all gender identities, while ensuring comfort and convenience for senior citizens through automatic lower berth allocation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a railway reservation system that not only improves the passenger experience, but also reflects the diversity of India’s travelers. By focusing on inclusivity, accessibility, and user comfort, we aim to set a new benchmark in railway booking services.
